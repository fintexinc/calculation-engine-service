GIC AND CASH HANDLING IN CALCULATION ENGINE
============================================

OVERVIEW
--------
GIC and Cash are self-contained holding types. Unlike stocks or funds,
they do not require external data lookups - all information comes from
the request itself.


GIC REQUEST FIELDS
------------------
  value           - Investment amount (required)
  clientIntRate   - Annual interest rate (required)
  term            - Term in days (required)
  currency        - Currency, defaults to CAD
  investmentDate  - When GIC was purchased
  interestFreq    - How often interest compounds (Annual, Monthly, etc.)


CASH REQUEST FIELDS
-------------------
  value           - Cash amount (required)
  currency        - Currency (required if multiple cash holdings)
  clientIntRate   - Interest rate (optional, for interest-bearing accounts)
  investmentDate  - When cash was acquired
  interestFreq    - How often interest compounds


INTEREST FREQUENCY OPTIONS
--------------------------
Annual, Semi-Annual, Quarterly, Bi-Monthly, Monthly, Bi-Weekly, Weekly, Daily


PROCESSING FLOW
---------------
1. Request received with GIC/Cash holdings
2. Validation checks:
   - GIC must have interest rate and term
   - Cash must have currency if there are multiple cash holdings
3. No external data lookup needed (self-contained)
4. Monthly returns calculated from interest rate and compounding frequency
5. Results returned


CALCULATIONS
------------
Monthly Returns:
  - Generated from investment date to today
  - Based on interest rate and compounding frequency

Asset Allocation (GIC only):
  - Short-term GIC (under 1 year): classified as Cash
  - Long-term GIC (1 year or more): classified as Fixed Income

Yield:
  - Uses the provided interest rate directly
